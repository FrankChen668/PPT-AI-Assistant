## SIE Rules
- Cover/chapter pages use dark backgrounds (#2C3E50 or #4A5558) with light text.
- Content pages use light backgrounds (#F2F6F6) and white cards (#FFFFFF).
- Use blue-gray separators (#7C969D) to build structure in place of excessive whitespace.
- Data pages are table-first. Charts are secondary and should keep full context labels.
- Table defaults: header #4A5558 with white text, odd rows #F2F6F6, even rows #FFFFFF, border #B4C6CA, highlight #EFDDE2.
